## Requirements and key decisions

Extend in-app reminders with opt-in APNs registration and dispatch, reschedule/deletion invalidation, deduplication and safe logout/account switching. Preserve bilingual iOS and the shared lead-time/read/snooze/cancel rules. Gmail/Outlook verification stays deferred. See [current behavior and setup](../../ios-push-acceptance.md).

Use live schedules, per-user transactions, durable delivery claims, absolute session expiry/auth version and installation sequence tombstones. Local logout happens before network work; cookie-free revocation retries after reconnect. Alerts contain no company, role, email body or credential. Taps require current authenticated server validation; cold taps wait for session restoration.

HTTP/2 APNs uses ES256 signing, cached provider tokens, zero offline expiration and stable schedule collapse IDs. Only explicit temporary rejections retry with backoff, at most three times. Unknown transport/crash outcomes are not replayed because Apple may already have accepted the alert. Already-accepted alerts cannot be guaranteed to disappear immediately from an offline device. Generic content, pre/post-send checks, foreground cleanup and server-authorized taps protect this boundary; exactly-once display and instant remote withdrawal are not promised.
