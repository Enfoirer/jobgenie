## Requirements and key decisions

The user requested an iOS survey with overall satisfaction and friend-recommendation ratings out of five stars, optional written feedback, Not now and permanent suppression, saved to the database for later analysis. Invitation targeting/timing were delegated, with emphasis on a good experience and reduced bias.

Use the same neutral questions for all users, without default stars, incentives or sentiment/outcome-based selection. Require seven days since first observed use and three recent UTC active dates, then two foreground minutes and a quiet root screen. Offer at most twice per campaign, defer at least 14 days after Not now and persist Never across accounts/devices correctly. Voluntary feedback remains available before eligibility/after opting out. Preserve response source, offered/shown/dismissed invitations and experience context. Five-point recommendation is not NPS; experienced-user and nonresponse bias remain explicit.

The backend derives ownership/source, validates and bounds input, checks origin/rate limits, and transactionally retains one immutable response per account/campaign. Device-scoped pending choices synchronize after reconnect. Demo data is isolated. See [policy and acceptance](../../ios-feedback-acceptance.md).

## Boundaries and follow-up

Only iOS has the survey UI; the shared backend is deployed separately. No analysis dashboard, App Store rating flow, APNs solicitation or automated third-party text analysis was added. Offline choices cannot instantly suppress another offline device; they synchronize after connection. Keep future campaign migrations from clearing permanent suppression. Maintain English records and the same original workbook after each delivery.
